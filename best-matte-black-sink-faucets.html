Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Brass bodies last longer than zinc alloy. Every faucet in this guide uses a brass body under the matte finish, which resists corrosion better than the hollow zinc faucets sold at the same price online.
  • The coating matters more than the color. A PVD (physical vapor deposition) black finish resists scratches and fingerprints far better than a sprayed-on paint finish, which chips at the edges within months.
  • Check your sink's hole count before you buy. Single-hole and 4-inch centerset are the two most common layouts among the best matte-black sink faucets, and swapping between them can mean drilling a new hole.
  • Spout height changes daily comfort. A taller gooseneck spout gives you room to wash your hands or rinse a razor without bumping your knuckles on the basin.
  • Price does not always predict quality. Our top pick costs $23.59 and beat several $50-plus faucets in flow and finish during testing.

How We Compared

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a matte-black sink faucet better than a chrome one?

A matte-black finish hides water spots and fingerprints far better than polished chrome, so you spend less time wiping down the faucet between cleanings. The tradeoff is that the black coating can scratch or fade if it's a cheap paint job rather than a PVD or powder-coat finish, which is why we prioritized coating quality across every pick in this guide to the best matte-black sink faucets.

Do matte-black faucets scratch or fade over time?

A well-made matte-black faucet holds its finish for years, but quality varies a lot between listings. Faucets with a PVD (physical vapor deposition) or powder-coat finish resist scratches and fading, while cheaper sprayed-on coatings can chip within months. We wiped every faucet in this test daily for three weeks, and only the budget pick showed the first faint signs of wear.

Can I install a matte-black sink faucet myself?

Most single-handle matte-black faucets, including every pick in this guide, install with basic tools in under thirty minutes if your sink already has the right hole count and spacing. Check whether your vanity uses a single hole or a 4-inch centerset spread before you buy, since matching that layout is what makes a DIY install go smoothly.
