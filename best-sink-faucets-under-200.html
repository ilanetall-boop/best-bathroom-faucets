Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Brass bodies cost more but last longer. Every full-size faucet in this guide uses a brass body rather than the zinc-alloy or plastic construction found in the cheapest listings, which is the main reason the best sink faucets under $200 tend to sit well above the $20 shelf.
  • Finish changes the price more than function does. Matte black and brushed gold, like the Delta Nicoli and Delta Arvo here, cost more than plain chrome mostly because of the finish, not because the faucet works any differently.
  • Check your mounting before you shop. A single-handle faucet like the Moen Revyl needs one hole, while a centerset model like the second Fransiton pick needs three holes spaced 4 inches apart. Measure your sink first so you do not buy a faucet you cannot install.
  • Not everything on this list is a full faucet. The SKYROKU and Fansoftiks picks are inexpensive extenders that attach to your existing faucet to help kids reach the sink on their own, and we have included them for households shopping for a full faucet upgrade alongside kid-friendly accessories.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best sink faucet under $200?

Based on our comparison of brass construction, finish durability, and brand support, the Delta Nicoli Matte Black at $184.80 is the best sink faucet under $200 for most bathrooms. If you would rather spend less, the Moen Revyl Chrome delivers the same brass reliability for $58.17.

Do I need a brass faucet, or is a cheaper material fine?

Brass matters more than almost any other spec in this price range. It resists corrosion and mineral buildup far better than the zinc-alloy or plastic internals found in the cheapest listings, which is why every full-size faucet in this guide, from the $184.80 Nicoli down to the $23.98 Fransiton, is built on a brass body.

Are the faucet extenders in this guide actual faucets?

No. The SKYROKU and Fansoftiks picks are inexpensive attachments that clip onto an existing faucet to help young kids reach the sink on their own. We included them because shoppers looking for a family-friendly sink upgrade often want both a new faucet and this kind of accessory, but they do not replace a full faucet.

How do I know if a faucet will fit my sink?

Count the holes drilled into your sink or countertop and measure the spacing between them. A single-hole faucet like the Moen Revyl needs only one opening, a 4-inch centerset like the second Fransiton pick needs three holes spaced 4 inches apart, and other configurations need wider spacing. Always check before you buy, since returning a faucet that does not fit is more hassle than measuring up front.

Is it worth spending close to $200 on a bathroom sink faucet?

It can be, if the finish and design matter to you. Our top pick, the Delta Nicoli Matte Black, costs more mainly because of its matte finish and brand backing, not because it functions dramatically better than a $58 Moen. If pure function and reliability are all you care about, the Revyl covers the same brass durability for a fraction of the price.
