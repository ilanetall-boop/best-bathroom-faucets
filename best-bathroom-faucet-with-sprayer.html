Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Sprayer design: On a sprayer faucet, the wand docks in the spout and pulls down on a hose, so you can direct water at the basin walls instead of splashing it there by hand.
  • Hole count: Match the faucet to your sink deck. This guide includes single-hole, 4-inch centerset, three-hole, and 8-inch widespread options, and none of them fit a layout they were not made for.
  • Under-sink clearance: The sprayer hose needs open space below the deck to drop and retract, so check that storage bins or plumbing do not crowd the area.
  • Finish: Brushed nickel dominates this list because it hides water spots and fingerprints better than polished chrome, which matters double on a fixture you grab with wet hands.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a special sink for a bathroom faucet with sprayer?

No, but you need to match the hole count. The Delta Arvo 1 Hole Pull fits single-hole sinks, the Ultimate Unicorn covers 4-inch centerset layouts, and the KENES and FGKQ picks fit three-hole decks. Count the holes in your sink before you order.

Is a pull-down sprayer worth it in a bathroom?

A pull-down sprayer lets you rinse toothpaste and shaving cream off the basin walls, wash your hair at the sink, and fill containers that do not fit under a fixed spout. If you clean your bathroom sink more than once a week, the wand pays for itself in saved effort.

How much should I spend on a bathroom faucet with sprayer?

Our picks run from $33.99 for the FGKQ to $150.38 for the Delta Arvo. Spend near $50 for a solid budget option like the BRAVEBAR, and step up to the Delta Arvo when you want brand parts support and a finish that holds up for years.

Does the sprayer hose need extra room under the sink?

Yes. A pull-down wand trails a hose that drops below the faucet body when you extend it, so clear a hand's width of space under the deck before install. Storage bins pressed against the plumbing can pinch the hose and shorten its life.
