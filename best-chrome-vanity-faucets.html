What is the best chrome vanity faucet for most bathrooms?

Do chrome faucets show water spots more than matte finishes?

Yes. Polished chrome reflects light beautifully, which also means hard-water spots and fingerprints show up more than they do on matte black or brushed nickel. The upside is that chrome wipes clean in seconds with a dry microfiber cloth, and it never looks dated. If you have very hard water, keep a cloth near the sink.

How do I know whether I need a centerset or widespread chrome faucet?

Count the holes in your sink and measure the spacing. A single-hole or 4-inch centerset faucet fits sinks with one hole or three holes spaced 4 inches apart. A widespread faucet needs three holes spaced 8 inches apart, center to center. There is no adapter that converts one layout to the other, so measure before you order.
