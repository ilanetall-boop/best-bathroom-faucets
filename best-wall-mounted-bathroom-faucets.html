Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Widespread and centerset are not interchangeable. A widespread faucet needs three separate holes spaced apart, while a centerset faucet needs a single 4-inch base plate. Measure your sink before you shop for wall-mounted bathroom faucets.
  • Every faucet in this guide uses a brass body under its visible finish, which is what actually determines how long it lasts, not the finish color.
  • Brushed nickel and matte finishes hide water spots and fingerprints better than polished chrome or bright brass, an easy way to cut down on wiping.
  • A recognized brand like Delta costs more, but it buys you easier access to replacement parts and a longer warranty than an unbranded import.
  • Price alone does not predict quality here. Our cheapest pick and our most expensive pick both carry the same 4-star average rating.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a widespread and a centerset wall-mounted bathroom faucet?

A widespread faucet has three separate pieces, the spout and two handles, connected under the sink and spaced across three separate holes. A centerset faucet mounts all three controls on a single base plate that covers a standard three-hole layout. Check your existing faucet's hole spacing before buying either style.

Are brand-name faucets like Delta worth the extra cost?

For most buyers, yes, if you plan to keep the faucet for years. A recognized brand like Delta makes it easier to find replacement cartridges and aerators later, and its warranty typically covers more than an unbranded import. If you are outfitting a rental or a short-term flip, one of the lower-priced brass picks in this guide covers the basics for less.

Do all the faucets in this guide fit a standard bathroom sink?

Most do, but you need to match the installation type to your sink first. The widespread picks need three separate holes spaced apart, while the centerset picks need a single base plate over one three-hole opening. Measure your current setup before ordering any of the best wall-mounted bathroom faucets listed here.
