Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Pfister Venturi widespread faucet worth the price?

At $128.78, the Pfister Venturi costs more than the Delta Arvo and both Charmingwater touchless faucets in this comparison, but it is built from solid brass rather than the composite bodies common at this price. For a traditional two-handle widespread faucet, that construction is the main reason to choose it over the cheaper alternatives.

What is the difference between the Pfister Venturi and a touchless faucet like the Charmingwater?

The Venturi uses standard handles that you turn to control water flow and temperature, while the Charmingwater models use a motion sensor so you can turn the water on and off without touching the faucet. Owners who want a hands-free, hygienic setup tend to prefer the touchless route, while buyers who want a simple, traditional faucet with brass construction lean toward the Venturi.

Does an 8 inch widespread faucet fit any bathroom sink?

No. Widespread faucets like the Pfister Venturi need a sink or countertop with three separate holes spaced to match the faucet's center-to-center measurement, typically around 8 inches for this style. If your sink has a single hole or different spacing, a widespread faucet will not install without modifying the countertop.
