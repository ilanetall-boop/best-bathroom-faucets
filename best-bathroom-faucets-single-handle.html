Frequently Asked Questions

Are single-handle bathroom faucets better than two-handle ones?

Single-handle faucets let you set temperature and flow with one lever, which is faster and keeps the deck cleaner. Two-handle models give you separate hot and cold control that some people find more precise. For most bathrooms, a single-handle faucet like our Peerless pick is the simpler daily choice.

Will a single-hole faucet fit my sink?

Check how many holes your sink has. Single-hole faucets need one hole, while centerset models like the Pfister Willa cover a 4-inch three-hole layout. Measure before ordering, since a centerset faucet will not fit a single-hole basin and a single-hole faucet leaves gaps on a three-hole sink.

Which faucet finish hides water spots best?

Brushed nickel with a spot-resistant coating, like Moen's Spot Resist on the Wellton and Karis, hides fingerprints and water marks best. Matte black also conceals spots well. Polished chrome looks bright but shows marks sooner, so plan on wiping it more often.

How much should I spend on a single-handle bathroom faucet?

Our picks run from $28.99 to $94.99. A $30 to $40 faucet like the Peerless covers most bathrooms well. Spend more only if you want a specific finish, a designer shape, or brand support like Moen's parts network.
