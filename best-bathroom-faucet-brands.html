How do I know which faucet spread size fits my sink?

Count the holes in your current sink or vanity top and measure the distance between the centers of the outer holes. A single hole takes a one-piece faucet, a 4-inch centerset has three holes close together, and an 8-inch widespread has three holes spaced further apart, like the Homevacious pick in this guide. Measuring first prevents the most common return reason for online faucet orders: a faucet that looks right in photos but does not match the existing holes.
