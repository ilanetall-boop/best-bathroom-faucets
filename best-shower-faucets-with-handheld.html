The Competition

We passed over shower faucet sets that only include a handheld wand with no fixed head option at all. A genuine shower faucet with handheld needs both fixtures working together, one for hands-free rinsing and one for targeted control, and a wand-only kit forces you to buy a second fixed head separately.

We also set aside all-plastic combo heads priced under $30 that do not list a brass or metal valve body. Every pick that made this guide, from the Aolemi rain set to the Delta In2ition, lists a brass-plus-finish construction, and a plastic body is one of the more common reasons a shower valve starts to leak or crack within a year or two.

Finally, we skipped listings that duplicate an already-included finish without a clear price or feature reason to include both. The two Delta Arvo 14 Series Matte listings in this guide made the cut because the price gap between them is worth flagging for anyone comparison shopping, but a third near-identical matte listing at a similar price would not have added anything new.

Weighing all of that, the best shower faucets with handheld in this lineup is still the Delta Arvo 14 Series Matte at $199.95 for anyone who wants the most complete kit, while the Delta 6-Setting In2ition 2-in-1 Dual at $59.99 is the stronger call if price is the deciding factor.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a 2-in-1 shower faucet with handheld and a standalone handheld wand?

A 2-in-1 system like the Delta 6-Setting In2ition or the Moen Engage Chrome Magnetix mounts a fixed head and a handheld wand on the same arm, so both fixtures share one water line and one valve. A standalone handheld replaces the fixed head entirely with just the wand and its bracket, which is a different fix if you want to keep hands-free rinsing as an option.

Do I need a plumber to install a shower faucet with handheld?

Most of the picks in this guide, including the Aolemi Shower Faucet Set Rain and both Delta Arvo 14 Series listings, are designed to thread onto an existing shower arm without opening the wall or replacing the valve behind it. If your current setup already has a single shower arm and no separate handheld line, installation is usually a wrench-and-tape job rather than a plumbing project. Homes without an existing shower arm are the exception.

Is Magnetix worth paying more for?

If you shower with kids, pets, or anyone who tends to drop the handheld wand back into its holder without looking, the magnetic docking on the Moen Engage Magnetix 6-Function and the Moen Engage Chrome Magnetix 2-in-1 solves a small but real daily annoyance. If you are the only person using the shower and you already aim the wand back into its bracket without issue, the non-magnetic Delta picks in this guide accomplish the same core function for less.
