Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Count your sink holes before you shop. A single-hole faucet like our FORIOUS top pick fits the most common bathroom sink, but a widespread sink with three holes spaced 8 inches apart, like the one the Pfister Courant is built for, needs a different faucet entirely. Measure first so you are not returning a box.
  • Brass beats zinc alloy at this price. A solid brass body resists corrosion at the threads and base far longer than the zinc used in the cheapest faucets. Most of the faucets we recommend here use brass, which is the biggest single factor in how long a budget faucet lasts.
  • Two of these seven picks are not faucets at all. The SKYROKU and Fansoftiks products are extenders that clip onto a faucet you already own so a toddler can reach the water. They solve a real problem for under $10, but they will not fix a leaking faucet or an ugly finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I spend on a sink faucet?

Most of the best sink faucets under $100 fall between $18 and $65. Our top pick, the FORIOUS Bathroom Faucet 1 Hole, costs $53.99 and uses a brass body rather than the zinc alloy common at the low end. Below about $15 you are usually buying a lighter faucet with a plastic cartridge, and above $100, as with the $147.87 Delta Arvo, you are mostly paying for a brand name rather than a faucet that performs noticeably better day to day.

Is a faucet extender a real substitute for a new faucet?

No, and we do not treat it as one. A faucet extender like the SKYROKU or the Fansoftiks clips onto your existing spout and redirects water down toward a toddler's hands; it does not fix a leaking valve or a worn finish. Buy an extender when your current faucet works fine and you only need your kid to reach it, and buy a full faucet, such as the Casavilla or Pacific Bay Lynden, when the faucet itself is the problem.

Will a single-hole faucet fit my three-hole sink?

Only with a deck plate rated for your sink's hole spacing. A single-hole faucet like our FORIOUS pick is built for one opening, so if your sink has a standard 4-inch centerset with three holes, you need a faucet that ships with a cover plate or one designed for that spread. An 8-inch widespread sink needs a true widespread faucet, which is why the Pfister Courant is the only widespread option in this roundup.

Can I install one of these faucets myself?

Most people can. A single-handle faucet like the FORIOUS or the Casavilla takes about 30 to 45 minutes with a basin wrench, plumber's tape, and some patience. The Pfister Courant, a widespread design, takes longer because you connect three separate pieces and their supply lines instead of one. The extenders are the easiest of all: both the SKYROKU and the Fansoftiks thread on by hand in a couple of minutes.
